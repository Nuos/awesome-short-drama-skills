**Topic description, briefly:**
- **Genre:** Historical romance (古言), set in a marquis's household, with family-scheming face-slapping, a hidden identity, divorce, and the husband regretting it too late (火葬场).

Each one's secret sits in one of her pawn tickets.
- **Core conflict:** The household wants to bleed her dry, but its finances all run through her counter.

- **Where the satisfaction comes from:**
  - Her identity comes out in four stages.
  - Her pawn tickets tell her everyone's secrets.
  - She spots fakes in a few lines.
  - Opponents lose to pawnshop rules they signed up to.
  - The husband ends up pawning the household's nameplate, and she marks it as never to be redeemed.
